The problem

Everyone serious about AI now runs three subscriptions and guesses.

ChatGPT for this, Claude for that, Gemini for the rest — decided by hand, every time. Three forces made that worse this year, not better.

Trust

Providers reroute you without asking.

OpenAI's undisclosed model-switching for "sensitive" conversations drew public backlash in 2026. People want to choose which model answers — and understand why — not have it decided behind the scenes.

Procurement

BYOK stopped being optional.

Buyers now ask who holds the key and how easily they can leave before adopting any AI tool at all — a power-user preference has become a baseline requirement.

Access

Smart routing exists — for developers only.

RouteLLM, Not Diamond, OpenRouter's Auto Router are real routing intelligence. All of it is an API for engineers to embed. None of it is something a person can simply open and use.
